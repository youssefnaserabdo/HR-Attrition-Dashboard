# 👥 HR Attrition Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
The **HR Attrition Dashboard** delivers a clear, data-driven view of workforce health, helping HR and business leaders quickly spot key risks and opportunities.

It highlights overall **headcount**, **attrition rates**, and **patterns across departments, salaries, experience, and distance from home**, revealing where turnover is highest and which factors—like tenure, promotion waits, or salary hikes—drive employees to leave.

These insights enable smarter decisions on **compensation**, **career development**, and **retention strategies** to reduce attrition and protect top talent.

---

## 🖼️ Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Overview Page
The main overview page of the HR Attrition Dashboard built in Power BI.  
It highlights key KPIs such as:  
- **1233 Current Employees**  
- **237 Total Attrition**  
- **16.1% Attrition Rate**

This page also features a variety of interactive visuals, including: 
- **Scatter Plot:** Average Monthly Income & Total Attrition Employees by Department & Experience.
- **Pie Charts:** Attrition vs Active Employees by Department.
- **Line Charts:** Total Active & Attrition Employees + Distance From Home.
- **Sunburst:** Attrition by Marital Status & Gender.
- **Decomposition Tree:** Employee Attrition Analysis.
- **Slicers:** Salary Segmentation & Age Segmentation.
A comprehensive view designed to help HR teams analyze employee turnover trends and identify the key factors driving retention.

### 2️⃣ Attrition Analysis Page
The Attrition Analysis page provides deeper insights into the key factors impacting employee retention and turnover.

Key highlights:  
- **7.01 Average Tenure** (Years) 
- **2.19 Average Promotion Wait** (Years) 
- **15.21% Average Salary Hike**

Interactive visuals include:  
- **Key Influencers:** Factors Affecting Employee Retention & Attrition.
- **Funnel with Source:** Total Attrition Employees by Job Role and Age Segmentation.
- **Waterfall Chart:** Total Attrition Employees by Salary Segmentation.
- **Column Chart:** Total Attrition Employees by Experience.
This analysis page enables HR teams to uncover patterns, identify high-risk groups, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.
